The turn rule CORRECTED 2026-07-22, 2nd PASS

  • Sign is FIXED to the deal, not the version: the party that did NOT send the deal card is the signer for the deal's whole life. Seller sent → buyer signs; buyer sent → seller signs. Changes during negotiation NEVER flip the sign right (→ B6).
  • Negotiate = talk, never delete: pressing Negotiate jumps to the chat, posts a pill ("[Name] wants to negotiate") and shows a strip at the TOP of the card ("In negotiation"). A held proposal STAYS on the table while people talk - it leaves only when withdrawn, replaced by the proposer's next Send, or accepted.
  • Replacement rule: while a proposal is on the table, its PROPOSER can edit and re-Send - the new version replaces the old proposal. The other side does not edit meanwhile.
  • Decline deal: always available to BOTH sides.
  • Pencil (edit): available to both sides when no change is held. The pencil is the ONLY editor.
  • Resolving a held change stays two-sided: if the deal SENDER must resolve a change (the signer proposed it), the sender gets "Accept changes" - commits the new version but does NOT sign; the signer then signs the updated card.
  • Send = implicit yes at both levels (sending the deal / sending a change).
  • The deal receiver (the fixed signer) ALWAYS has three options: Negotiate / Sign / Decline - including on the very first version (B1).
  • The signer never signs their own unaccepted proposal: while the signer's OWN proposal is on the table, their Sign button stays VISIBLE but DISABLED ("Waiting for [name]'s acceptance") and activates only after the other side presses Accept changes. Consequence: a buyer-sent deal always takes ONE EXTRA STEP (seller sends changes → buyer accepts → seller signs); a seller-sent deal signs directly (buyer's Sign = accept + sign in one).
  • Product reality (Ayush): buyers mostly do NOT edit the card - most edit features are seller-only anyway. Buyers negotiate through CHAT; the seller makes the versions, in BOTH deal directions.
  • Buyer-initiated (order) mirror: buyer builds the order (prices may stay empty - seller-owned pricing), sends it; the SELLER is the fixed signer; the seller fills prices via Send-changes; the BUYER gets "Accept changes"; after accept, the seller's Sign activates and they sign. DECIDED: a buyer create-door WILL be built (→ C5). A1 keeps the machinery direction-neutral.
