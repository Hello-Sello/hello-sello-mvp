Deal acceptance & negotiation - the CURRENT flow

This is exactly what the deal card does right now - drawn from the live decisionBar.ts matrix + sign_deal / confirm_deal_change RPCs. Read each step and check: is the button on the correct side? Is the round order right? The yellow ❓ CHECK boxes mark places worth a second look.

How to read this

Sign the deal / Accept changes = primary (filled) Negotiate / Withdraw = secondary (outline) disabled Sign = shown but greyed Decline deal = always there (both sides)
SIGNER = the FIXED signer = the side that did NOT send the deal SENDER = the side that sent the deal SELLER owns prices BUYER
  • The signer is fixed for the deal's whole life - it is the receiver (the side that did NOT press Send). A mid-deal change never flips it.
  • Send = an implicit "yes" at both levels (sending the deal, and sending a change).
  • The signer never signs their own un-accepted change - their Sign shows but is disabled ("Waiting for [them]'s acceptance") until the sender presses Accept changes.
  • Negotiate never deletes anything - it jumps to chat, posts a "wants to negotiate" pill + an "In negotiation" strip; the held change stays on the table.
  • A change is proposed with the pencil (edit the card → "Send changes"), not a bar button. The proposer can Withdraw or edit-and-re-send (replace).
  • After Confirmed (signed), the seller uploads an invoice → the deal goes to Done.

Exits available in every round (both scenarios)

Decline deal (either side, any round) → a two-click confirm → CANCELLED (a close, not a delete).
Withdraw changes (only the proposer of the held change) → removes their held change; the deal returns to the last agreed version.
Negotiate (only the signer's bar) → opens the chat, posts a "wants to negotiate" pill + an "In negotiation" strip; nothing on the card is discarded.
