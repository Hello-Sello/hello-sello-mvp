Locked decisions

  • Default tab: opening Connect lands on Chat, not Inbox (/connect → /connect/chat).
  • Empty chat state: Chat opens with no conversation selected — just a blank pink background. The thread opens only when you tap a conversation (WhatsApp-style). No thread auto-loads.
  • Rename: "Workspace" → "Deal Room".
  • The "AI" pill is fake - cosmetic only, no engine behind it. So your idea of a fixed Sella tile that holds the notification + Review-change feed is the clean path; nothing real breaks.
  • Inbox rename: parked on purpose - we name it once the Sella-tile design lands (same idea at two scales).
