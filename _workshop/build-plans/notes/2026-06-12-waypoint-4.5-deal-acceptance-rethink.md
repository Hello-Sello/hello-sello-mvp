## The observation

Two people are just chatting, no deal yet. A deal can start two ways:
1. **Sella detects** one (auto-detection → asks both to accept → deal is born).
2. **A person creates** one themselves (the "No deal yet → Start a deal" bar, or the composer `+` → "Create a
   deal").

In every case, **acceptance currently lives ON the deal card** (the `ConfirmBar` in `CardFront`). That is the
root of three knots.

## The three knots

1. **Accepting your own deal is wrong.** When a *person* creates the deal, the card then shows *them* an
   accept/decline. You do not accept a deal you just made. It clutters the card.
   → **Remove accept/decline from the deal card.** (Same instinct as Ayush's earlier note: keep the card clean,
   move the action to notifications.)

2. **Orphan workspaces.** Birth creates the **deal workspace in the same transaction** as the card. If the two
   sides never confirm, there is an **unnecessary deal workspace** sitting around.
   → **Do not create the workspace until acceptance happens.** Defer workspace birth to after both accept.

3. **The Sella contradiction (the buggy core).** When Sella *detects*, Sella must get acceptance from both
   people. But acceptance is on the card, AND **Sella may not create a card** (the AI fence - L1 suggest only).
   So Sella literally has no allowed place to ask for acceptance. The "accept on the card" design and the AI
   fence are in direct conflict.
   → **Acceptance must live OUTSIDE the card** so Sella can request it without making a card.

## Ayush's direction (resolve around this)

- **Move acceptance into NOTIFICATIONS.** Two homes:
  - the **top notification bell** (already placeholdered in 5A.2), and
  - **inside the chat, a "Sella notification" / Sella's presence** - Sella posts the acceptance request there.
- **Sella sends the acceptance request as a notification, not a card.** Both people accept from the
  notification.
- **Birth happens only AFTER both accept** - the card (and the workspace) are born then, so no orphan
  workspace and no "accept your own deal" weirdness.
- **Sella's presence in the chat matters "for growth"** - start giving Sella a visible presence in the chat
  (the notification area is the first form of it). Later, think about where else to place Sella's presence.
- This keeps the **card clean** (it just shows the deal) and resolves the fence-vs-acceptance conflict.
