Deal Card - S2 redesign

Throwaway prototype for slice S2 "The Card". Three different directions for the same deal, all on the real pink palette. Target from screenshot SS-3: clean, professional, compact - "DEAL - id" header, the "On DATE X offered Y this deal" line, product lines with tiny thumbnails, Decline / Change / Accept, a Message box, a small "Things" checklist. Open the file, scan all three, pick one (or mix).

Variation A — Compact ledger

Reads like a clean invoice. Everything shows at once - parties, every product line, terms grid, total, your margin, the Things checklist. No tabs, no expand. Best when the buyer wants the whole deal on one calm surface and the product count is small (2-4 lines).

A — what shows

  • Default: everything. Parties, all product lines, terms grid, total, margin, Things.
  • Hidden: nothing - flip side (Signals / Logs) holds the advisory + history.
  • Edit + flip: top corners, same idiom as today.
  • Best for short deals. Gets tall with many lines.

Variation B — Thumbnail-led, terms collapse

Products are the hero: bigger thumbnails, THC/CBD as coloured tags, the thing a procurement buyer scans first. The dry terms (delivery, payment, margin) collapse behind a "Deal terms" line so the card stays short by default. The total sits in a bold pink bar. Best when "what am I buying" matters more than the fine print.

B — what shows

  • Default: the story line, all products (hero), total bar, Things chips, actions.
  • Hidden (tap to open): dry terms + your margin behind "Deal terms".
  • Edit + flip: top corners.
  • Stays short even with many lines; the buyer scans products fast.

Variation C — Sectioned / tabbed

A deep-pink header carries identity + the headline number, then three segments - Products / Terms / Things - with one open at a time. The shortest card: only one section's detail is on screen, the rest is one tap away. Best when deals are large (many lines, many checklist items) and the card must not dominate the chat strip.
