**Question:** What should the `//` Sella mark look like? The build plan asks to
change the AI logo to a **`//` double-slash with a "bubbly AI feel"**, reused
**everywhere** Sella shows up (chat avatar, the fixed strip tile, the right
panel header, inline system lines) and that **lights up in place** when a deal
change is pending. `//` is the He//o Se//o brand mark — fine for Sella, never for
the relationship mark.

## Shape of this prototype

A logo is judged in context, not in a vacuum — so the **4 context cards are a
constant harness** (real app chrome: chat bubbles, strip, panel, inline line).
The **"variant" is the mark itself** — switching swaps only the `//` treatment.
An **Idle ⇄ Change pending** toggle proves the "lights up in place" state.

The slashes are CSS-drawn skewed pills (not the glyph `//`), so roundness and
cap shape — the "bubbly" knob — are fully controllable.

## Round 1 variants

| Key | Name | The structural bet |
|---|---|---|
| A | Bubble badge | Soft squircle, maroon→pink gradient, white rounded slashes. App-icon / sticker feel. Lights up = gentle scale-pulse + glow ring. |
| B | AI orb | Round assistant orb with a slow rotating halo ring + glowing slashes. Most overtly "living AI". Lights up = halo speeds up + brightens. |
| C | Speech mark | The `//` in a chat-bubble shape with a little tail; two-tone ink+pink slashes. Editorial, brand-forward. Lights up = warm fill + tail ping. |

**How to run:** open `index.html` in a browser. Switch with the top bar or
`←` / `→`. Toggle **Idle / Change pending** (top-right) to see the lit state.
