Hello Sello rail - 3 models to compare

One global left rail, three ways it can show Connect's sub-items when you drill in. Open each model and play with the collapse toggle and the drill control.

1

Replace (drill-down)

Connect's sub-nav slides in and takes the place of the surface list - one level at a time.

 2

Accordion (tree)

Connect opens in place as a sub-tree under itself - siblings stay visible the whole time.

 3

Accordion + Messages

Same accordion tree, plus a Messages people-list below it - closest to the reference screenshots.

Source: Connect/Chat UI overhaul build plan, slice F2 (nav merge). See NOTES.md for the shared model and the recommendation.
