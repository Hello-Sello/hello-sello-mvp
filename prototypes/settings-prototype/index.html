Deactivate your account?

This is like taking a break. Your profile is hidden, you're signed out everywhere, and nothing is deleted. Next time you sign in, we'll ask if you want to reactivate.

Delete your account

This starts a permanent deletion (GDPR erasure). Your account is disabled straight away.
You have 30 days to change your mind. Sign in and cancel any time in that window. After 30 days your name, email and photo are permanently scrubbed — this can't be undone. Your company's audit history is kept in anonymized form, as the law requires.
