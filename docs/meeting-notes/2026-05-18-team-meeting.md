## Big 7 Framework — locked

`Connect — Buy — Sell — Present — Trade — Discover — Sella` (+ Home as landing page)

The original meeting note phrased the framework as "Seller (AI core) - Connect - Present - Sell - Buy - Trade - Discover" — confirmed 2026-05-19 by Muskan that "Seller" in that wording was a misname for **Sella** (Seller = the seller-side user role; Sella = the AI agent).

### Per-pillar one-liner (from the meeting table)

- **Connect** — Chat with every partner inside or outside Hello Sello — you are in, you win.
- **Buy** — Smart procurement decisions with visibility of all deals, prices and margins.
- **Sell** — Allocate your batches with amazing margin control.
- **Present** — Show what you've got, put it in a basket and send it as a deal(room). The online shop for sellers that is also the best presentation ever.
- **Trade** — A command center for all your deals.
- **Discover** — Find new interesting suppliers globally and get inspired from top level service providers — a network social feed. Legal advertisement for brands since the audience is verified and professionally allowed to see it (closed gang).
- **Sella** — A female-inspired caring AI for both sides, mediating for collaborative mutual benefits.
- **Home** — Start from website, login portal top-right, FIGMA design as UI base, pink replacing blue.

## Main focus: BUY — led by Victor Diem

Simulates buying price + additions + prescription fees, outputs sell price, DB calculation, and margin % in real time.

### 2. Deal Engine
Headline: **"more is the strategy"** — not just cutting prices but adding value on both sides. Key mechanics:
- **Shock drops** (think Nike limited releases — urgency + hype)
- **Volume deals** — buy more, unlock more
- **Short-expiry deals** — time pressure drives decisions
- **Bundles** — cross-product value stacking
- **Launch promos** — pre-configured for new product entries

### 3. Exclusivity Deals
Seller badges exclusive to Hello Sello — gives the platform real leverage.

### 4. Cash-Flow Calculator
Buyer-side financial clarity tool.

### 5. Product Data Bank
Market-wide product knowledge base.
