## What it closes

`product_basket_line` carried **one** policy — *"is this my line?"* — and nothing else. Any
authenticated caller could POST any `product_id`, a competitor's hidden product included, and the
row was admitted. T06 made it worse exactly as the ADR predicted: widening what a connected buyer
may *see* widened what they may *reference*, and the basket never consulted the visibility rule at
all.

**The design reuses that rule rather than restating it.** A policy subquery is RLS-filtered as the
calling role, so `exists (select 1 from product p where p.id = …)` inherits T06's rule for buyers
and `product_all` for sellers — **no predicate duplicated, and no edit here when the rule next
changes.** Only the price arm is new text.
